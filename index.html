Komikan

A Mapuche hunting game. One komikelu, “the one who eats everything”, against twelve trewas, the dogs that corner it.

How to play
  • The komikelu moves first. Then players take turns.
  • Every piece moves one point along a line, forwards or backwards.
  • The komikelu can eat a trewa by jumping over it in a straight line onto an empty point. It may keep jumping in the same turn, or stop.
  • Trewas can't jump or eat. They win by leaving the komikelu with no possible move.
  • The komikelu wins by eating 8 trewas.
  • Added for this version: a game still going after 200 turns is a draw.

Rules from Chile para Niños (CC BY-SA) and Matus (1920). Komikan is a game of the Mapuche people.
